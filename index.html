Two screens (recommended): open the Host view on your laptop screen and the Kids' board on the projector. The host view shows you every answer; the kids' board only shows what you reveal. They stay in sync as long as both are open in the same browser on the same computer.

How to run it: Read the question out loud. When a kid gives an answer that is on the board, click its tile (or press 1 to 5). Wrong answer? Hit Strike (or X). Pick the team that is playing first (the Playing buttons): every tile you flip adds its points to that team instantly, on their card and in the scoreboard. Three strikes? Switch to the other team by clicking Steal next to their name if their one guess is on the board; they take every point on it. Made a mistake? Undo last answer hides the tile and takes the points back, Redo this round starts the round over, and the scoreboard has manual +/- fixes. Scores and the scoreboard are visible on your host view only. Score numbers are hidden from the kids' board until you click Show scores on kids' board (good for a dramatic reveal). Start over wipes everything for the next class.
